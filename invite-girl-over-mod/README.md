# Invite Girl Over Mod

A mod for X-Change Life that allows male players to invite girlfriends over in the evening, mirroring the "Invite guy over" feature available to female players.

## Features

- Adds "Invite girl over" as an evening activity when playing as male
- Track relationships with girls who become "girlfriends"
- Call and invite girlfriends to your home
- Interact with visiting girlfriends (talk or intimate encounters)
- Option for them to stay the night
- Relationship progression (friendship, attraction)

## Installation

1. Copy the `invite_girl_over.twee` and `invite_girl_over.meta` files to your mods folder
2. The mod will automatically integrate with the game's activity system

## How It Works

The mod adds a "girlfriend" relationship tag system similar to the existing "fuckbuddy" system for male NPCs. When you build relationships with female NPCs (at bars, clubs, etc.) and they become interested in you, they can become girlfriends.

The "Invite girl over" option appears in the evening activities when:
- You are currently male
- You have at least one girlfriend in your relationships

## Compatibility

- Requires X-Change Life version 0.17f or later
- Overrides the `refresh possible activities relationships` passage to add girlfriend support while maintaining original fuckbuddy functionality
